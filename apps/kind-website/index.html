The problem we solve

Lots of health advice.
Very little clarity.

A flood of opinions, experts, supplements, and influencers — but it's almost impossible to know what will work for you, or to sustain change without the right support.

🌊

Information overload

Endless conflicting claims make it harder, not easier, to make good decisions about your health. Kind cuts through the noise with structured, personalised explorations.

🧩

One size doesn't fit all

What works for the population average may not work for you. Kind helps you run personalised explorations and see your individual results — not just group statistics.

🏃

Change is hard alone

Even motivated people struggle to sustain new habits without support. Kind provides an AI companion and community to help you follow through.

🔬

Research is slow and costly

Clinical trials take years and miss real-world nuance. Kind turns everyday individual exploration into evidence the research community can learn from.

How Kind works

Two communities,
one shared mission

Kind connects people who are exploring their health with experts who want to understand and improve it — creating a virtuous loop of knowledge and evidence.

For individuals

Your personal health journey

Whether you're trying to sleep better, manage energy, or explore the effect of diet on your wellbeing — Kind helps you do it with structure, support, and real insight.

  • Get matched with health explorations tailored to your goals
  • Log your habits and results with gentle AI reminders
  • See your personal results alongside group findings
  • Connect with a community on a similar path
  • Build on science — not just social media trends
Learn more →
For researchers

Real-world evidence at scale

Kind gives academics, advocates, and health experts a platform to share interventions, recruit participants, and generate publishable insights from real-world data.

  • Share curated health content and intervention protocols
  • Access anonymised data from willing individual explorers
  • Run citizen science explorations with consenting participants
  • Publish and share research findings from aggregated data
  • Build your profile as a trusted health authority
