Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Kind (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on kind-health.app and related marketing pages (the “Website”). It should be read together with our privacy information in the FAQ.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies (such as pixels, tags, and local storage) may also be used for comparable purposes. We refer to all of these as “cookies” in this policy.

Why we use cookies

  • Strictly necessary — to operate the Website, remember your cookie choices, and keep the site secure.
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use the Website (e.g. pages viewed, referral sources) so we can improve content and experience.
  • Marketing & advertising — to measure ad campaigns, attribute conversions, and run advertising tests (including remarketing where applicable).

Your choices (GDPR / UK GDPR / ePrivacy)

If you are in the UK or European Economic Area (or another jurisdiction with similar rules), we only set non-essential cookies after you give consent. You can:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Choose categories in Cookie preferences

You may withdraw or change your consent at any time via Cookie preferences in the site footer.

Cookie categories we use

Strictly necessary

These cookies are essential and cannot be disabled via our preference centre.

  • kind_cookie_consent — stores your cookie choices (local storage / similar, up to 12 months or until you clear site data).

Analytics (optional)

Only placed if you consent. May include services such as Google Analytics or comparable tools. Typical purposes: aggregated visit statistics, page performance, traffic sources. Retention depends on the provider (often up to 14–26 months for analytics identifiers).

Marketing & advertising (optional)

Only placed if you consent. Used for ad measurement and testing (e.g. Meta, Google Ads, LinkedIn, or other ad platforms we use). These cookies may track visits across sites to measure campaign performance and build audiences. Retention varies by provider; see their policies for details.

Third parties

Some cookies are set by third-party providers acting as separate controllers or processors. When we enable a provider, their use of data is governed by their privacy policy. We configure tags to respect your consent choices where technically possible (including Google Consent Mode where applicable).

How to control cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies via your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect site functionality. For guidance, see your browser’s help pages (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, etc.).
