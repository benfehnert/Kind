Alpha test

Kind Health Alpha — test day 1

Welcome to the start of the test. The app is now ready for you to use :)

Your task today is to add the app to your phone, go through onboarding and to start to use the app.

Step 1: Add the app to your phone

Open the Kind app link: https://web.kind-health.app/

If you are an iOS user:

  1. Open the Kind app link in Safari (unfortunately Chrome doesn’t support the app on iOS);
  2. Click the share icon on the bottom of Safari;
  3. Scroll down and click ‘Add to Home Screen’;
  4. Return to your home screen and click on the app icon.

If you are an Android user:

  1. Open the Kind app link in Chrome;
  2. Click the menu on the top right corner of Chrome;
  3. Click ‘Add to Home Screen’, unless Chrome prompts you to install the app;
  4. Return to your home screen and click on the app icon.

Step 2: Create an account

  1. Click on the Kind app icon on the home screen.
  2. Select ‘Create an account’.
  3. Follow the account setup and onboarding process.

Step 3: Start to use the app as you want

When the onboarding is complete, you’ll be taken to the home screen.

You can then have a look round the app and start to use it as you see fit.

Step 4: Give feedback

After using the app a little, please click on the ‘Give feedback’ button in the bottom right corner, and select ‘Daily feedback’. You will then be asked a few questions about your experience today.

Here you can also see the ‘Report issue’ button and you can use it to let us know of any bugs or issues you encountered, or any suggestion you have.

Important note

When we release the app, most health explorations will take between 6 to 8 weeks. As we don’t have time to do this during the Alpha test, we have shortened all of the health explorations, so each lasts just 6 to 8 days (1 day in these Short explorations, represents what would be 1 week in a Full exploration).
