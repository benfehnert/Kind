Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kind (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit kind-health.app, join our waitlist, or use the Kind health exploration platform (together, the “Services”). Kind has been tested in alpha with people in the United States, the United Kingdom, and the Netherlands. The public launch is planned for 2027, for adults aged 18 and over in the United States. This policy should be read together with our Cookie Policy.

Who we are

Kind is a health exploration platform that helps adults run structured, time-limited health explorations. Using Kind and contributing to research are separate choices. If you opt in, you can contribute de-identified results to research. Kind is a general wellness and research platform. It is not a medical service, does not give diagnosis or treatment advice, and is not a HIPAA covered entity. Kind follows the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and applicable state health data laws. Kind never sells your data and never uses health data for advertising.

Where Kind is available

Kind has been built and tested in alpha with users in the United States, United Kingdom, and the Netherlands during 2026. We plan to launch in the United States in 2027, starting with a soft launch for waitlist members and followed by a full US launch. At launch, Kind will be available to adults aged 18 and over in the United States. The waitlist is open more widely, and we will say when Kind becomes available in other places. If you access the Services, this Privacy Policy applies to you. The UK and Netherlands rights below still apply to people there who visit the site, join the waitlist, or whose information we already hold from alpha testing.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with Kind, we may collect:

  • Website and waitlist data — such as your name, email address, and any information you provide when joining a waitlist or contacting us.
  • Account and profile data — such as your display name, location, year of birth, and profile preferences you choose to provide in the app.
  • Health and exploration data — information you choose to log during explorations, including daily ratings, lifestyle notes, exploration outcomes, and adherence data.
  • Technical and usage data — such as device type, browser, IP address, pages viewed, and similar analytics (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Communications — messages you send to us and our responses.

We do not require you to provide health information to browse the Website. Health-related data is collected only when you choose to use the platform and give the relevant consent.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Operate the Website and platform, including waitlist management and account access
  • Deliver personalised health explorations and your individual (N-of-1) results
  • Send service updates, reminders, and support communications (where you have opted in)
  • Improve our Services, security, and user experience
  • Conduct research with de-identified data — only where you have given a separate research opt-in, and specific consent for each exploration
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

Legal bases (United Kingdom and Netherlands)

Where UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies — including if you are in the United Kingdom or the Netherlands — we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for optional uses such as research contribution, marketing cookies, and certain health data processing
  • Contract — to provide the Services you sign up for
  • Legitimate interests — to operate and improve the Website, prevent abuse, and communicate about the Services (balanced against your rights)
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information by law

United States privacy

If you are in the United States, Kind handles your personal information in line with applicable US federal and state privacy laws, including state comprehensive privacy laws and, where applicable, state consumer health data laws.

  • No sale of personal information — Kind does not sell your personal information or consumer health data.
  • Consumer health data — where you use the platform, health-related information you log may be treated as consumer health data under certain US state laws. We collect and use it only with your consent and for the purposes described in this policy and in our in-app consent flows.
  • Your choices — you can withdraw consent, update privacy settings in the app, and request access to or deletion of your personal information as described below.
  • Targeted advertising — we do not use your health or exploration data for cross-context behavioural advertising. Where state law gives you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising, that right is available to you; because we do not sell personal information, no separate sale opt-out is required for that activity.

Sharing your information

Kind does not sell your personal or consumer health data.

We may share information with:

  • Service providers — such as hosting, analytics, email, and waitlist tools, under contracts that require them to protect your data
  • Research partners — only if you separately opt in to research and give specific consent for an exploration. Shared data is de-identified to the HIPAA Safe Harbor standard. Researchers mostly see group-level results. Where they need more detail, they can access de-identified row-level data only under an agreement that bans any attempt to re-identify you or transfer the data onward.
  • Authorities — if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

Individual-level health data is never shared with researchers in identifiable form. You can withdraw research consent at any time. Your data will not be used in future analyses. Data already included in completed analyses cannot be taken back.

International transfers

Kind has tested with people in the United States, United Kingdom, and Netherlands, and plans a United States launch in 2027. Your information may be processed in these countries or in other locations where our service providers operate. Where UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or other transfer rules apply, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent measures) to protect data transferred internationally.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, to meet legal requirements, or to resolve disputes. Waitlist data is retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Platform data is retained according to your account status and consent choices.

Your rights and choices

Your rights depend on where you live. In all regions, you can contact us at hello@kind-health.app to exercise applicable rights.

United Kingdom and Netherlands

If you are in the United Kingdom or the Netherlands, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (the ICO in the UK, or the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands)

United States

If you are in the United States, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent for consumer health data collection or sharing, where you previously opted in
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights

In the Kind app, you can review and change consent, visibility, and research contribution settings from your profile at any time. You can download your exploration data. If you withdraw research consent, your data will not be used in future analyses. Data already included in completed analyses cannot be taken back.

Security

We apply technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Kind is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us data, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated by email or in-app notice where appropriate.
