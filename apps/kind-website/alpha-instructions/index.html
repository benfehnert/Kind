Kind Health Alpha — Instructions

Welcome to the Kind Health Alpha user test.

This is a 2-week usability test of the ‘Alpha’ prototype of the new Kind Health app: We would like you to use the app prototype to help us understand how we can make it better. We will guide you to explore different parts of Kind (with tasks taking less than 5 minutes a day), but we also encourage you to use it as much or as little as feels natural for you.

There will be a dedicated ‘Give feedback’ button that you can access at any point in the app. As you do different things in the app, we will ask you to use this button to complete short surveys about how easy to use and valuable you are finding a specific feature or the app in general. The ‘Give feedback’ button also allows you to report issues and bugs across the app as you encounter them.

Remember, flagging issues and sharing suggestions helps us make the app better, so please don’t feel a need to hold back on problem you encounter or things which do not work as you expect.

We will also be able to monitor your activity on the prototype using a service called PostHog, which allows us to see what you engage with when.

If you are interested in taking part, please respond directly to me before [date]. I will then share the task list and link to the app the day before the test starts.

Lastly, please note, the health explorations you will see in the Alpha app are all marked as (Short). In these each health exploration will run for only 1 day for each week of what would happen in a (Full) exploration.

How to install the app

The first step involves adding the app to your device using this link: https://kind-app-staging.ben-fehnert.workers.dev/

If you are an iOS user:

  1. Open the link in Safari (unfortunately Chrome doesn’t support the app on iOS);
  2. Click the share icon on the bottom of Safari;
  3. Scroll down and click ‘Add to Home Screen’;
  4. Confirm the name (Kind Health) and click ‘Add’;
  5. Return to your home screen and click on the app icon;
  6. The app should now open full-screen.

If you are an Android user:

  1. Open the link in Chrome;
  2. Click the menu on the top right corner of Chrome;
  3. Click ‘Add to Home Screen’, unless Chrome prompts you to install the app;
  4. Return to your home screen and click on the app icon;
  5. The app should now open full-screen.

After the app has been successfully added to your device, open the app.

Add your email and password to create an account.

Complete the onboarding process.

When the onboarding is complete, you’ll be taken to the home screen and can start using the app.
