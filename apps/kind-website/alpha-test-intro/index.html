Kind Health Alpha — test intro

Welcome to the Kind Health Alpha prototype test.

This is a 10 day test of the new Kind Health app prototype: from Tuesday 14 to Thursday 23 July.

During that time, we encourage you to use it as much or as little as feels natural for you. However, each day we will guide you to different parts of Kind and ask you to try different features.

There is also a ‘Give feedback’ button in the app. As you use the app, we will ask you to use this button to complete a short ‘Daily feedback’. You can also use the same ‘Give feedback’ button to ‘Report issues’ as you encounter them.

Please remember, issues and suggestions are most welcome as they will help us make the app better. Please don’t feel a need to hold back on criticism.

We will also be monitoring your activity using a service called PostHog, which allows us to see what you engage with.

In summary:

1. Daily tasks

Each morning, we will send a Whatsapp message and direct you to something we would like you to do on the Kind app.

2. Daily Feedback after each task

After you complete each task, we would like you to:

  1. Click on the ‘Give feedback’ button on the bottom right corner of the app.
  2. Select the ‘Daily Feedback’ option.
  3. Complete the survey and submit.

3. Reporting bugs or issues

At any point, you can also use the ‘Give feedback’ button to report other issues or bugs that you encounter:

  1. At any point, click on the ‘Give feedback’ button on the bottom corner of the app.
  2. Select the ‘Report issue’ option.
  3. Describe your issue and submit.

4. Monitoring activity

Over the test period, we will also be able to track your engagement through a platform called PostHog. This will allow us to see when you do what in the app, linked to your user account.

5. Follow up — after the end of the test

After day 10 we will be reviewing all the activity and feedback. At this point, we may also invite you to a short 30 minute interview to understand your views and how you found the app.
