Who Kind is for

Wherever you are on
your health journey

You don't need to be a biohacker or a fitness obsessive, but we imagine you are all keen to explore. Kind meets you where you are.

The optimizer

You're already experimenting with your health and want structured, evidence-backed protocols to take it further — and a kind community that you can share your journey with.

The improver

You've got health goals — better sleep, more energy, less stress — and you want trusted guidance and a gentle nudge to actually make progress.

The explorer

You've had a new diagnosis, received advice from a doctor, or simply want to understand your body better. Kind helps you take those first steps with confidence.

How Kind works

From curiosity to
real understanding

Kind walks alongside you through a structured exploration — from understanding your goals to seeing your personal results.

1

Tell Kind about you

Share your health goals, lifestyle, and what you've already tried. Kind builds a personal profile to match you with relevant explorations.

2

Choose an exploration

Browse curated protocols — like a caffeine window for sleep, or creatine for energy — and choose one that fits your life and goals.

3

Log and track

Your AI companion sends gentle reminders and helps you log what you're doing and how you're feeling — no complicated data entry required.

4

See your results

At the end of your exploration, see how it worked for you personally — and how your results compare to others in the group.

5

Follow the community

Connect with people on similar journeys, share your experience, and benefit from the collective wisdom of the Kind community.

6

Contribute to science

With your consent, your anonymised exploration data contributes to real research — helping build evidence that benefits everyone.

AI personalisation

Matched to you,
not the population average

Most health advice is based on what works for the average person. But you're not average. Kind's AI matches you with explorations based on your profile, goals, and what's already worked (or hasn't) for people like you.

  • Personal intake to understand your goals and health context
  • Matching with health explorations most relevant to you
  • Results - showing what works for you and what does not
  • Follow on options of further health explorations to suit you
AI companion

Support that keeps
you on track

Any change is hard — especially alone. Your Kind companion checks in with you, celebrates progress, helps you troubleshoot, and reminds you why you started. It's the accountability partner that never gets tired.

  • Gentle daily reminders tailored to your exploration schedule
  • Conversational check-ins to track how you're doing
  • Real-time support when you hit obstacles or want to quit
  • Insights surfaced at the right moment to keep you motivated
Community

You're not exploring
alone

Join groups of like-minded individuals exploring similar health topics. Share your experiences, follow others on their journeys, and build on collective insight — all in a kind and supportive space.

  • Join topic-based groups (e.g. Sleep, Energy, Gut Health)
  • Follow other explorers with similar profiles and goals
  • Be followed yourself and receive support and encouragement from others
  • See anonymised group results alongside your own
Your data, your control

Safe, private,
and transparent

Kind stores it securely in your personal data vault, where you can see it and see how it is used by researchers. We're built on HIPAA-compliant principles and robust data security.

  • Personal data vault — you own your health data
  • Full control and ability to download your data
  • Never identifiable - only anonymised data is shared
  • HIPAA compliance and best-practice data security
Launch explorations

Start with what
matters to you

Kind launches with a curated set of evidence-backed explorations. New areas are added as researchers contribute protocols and the community grows.

Sleep quality

Explore caffeine timing, evening screen exposure, sleep schedule consistency, and supplementation approaches like magnesium or L-theanine.

Energy and focus

Investigate creatine supplementation, intermittent fasting windows, HRV-guided training, and stimulant timing for sustainable energy.

Diet and gut health

Track the impact of dietary changes on how you feel — from fibre and fermented foods to elimination approaches and microbiome support.

Mental wellbeing

Explore mindfulness practices, screen time reduction, breathwork, and other lifestyle-based interventions for mood and stress.

Heart and metabolic health

Monitor the impact of exercise, diet, and lifestyle changes on blood pressure, heart rate variability, and metabolic markers.

More coming soon

As research partners contribute new explorations, Kind's library will grow — covering more areas of health and wellbeing.
