## Context

Kind System 2026.08.28 assigned Touchpoint 5 to "the same reviewer role as Touchpoint 4", which has no named owner. The R&R document doesn't assign it either. The analysis proposes the Head of Science, and the user agreed ("Agreed", `context/analysis.md`). The Head of Science already prepares each protocol against an approved template for the Protocol Review Board, and runs the review cycle "against approved templates and N-of-1 and trial reporting standards" (R&R p.8).

## Decision

The Head of Science makes the Kind-readiness determination (Touchpoint 5).

## Alternatives considered

None were proposed in the sources.

## Consequences

- **Docs.** §0.1 and §4.2.
- **Code.** None.
- **Process.** The Head of Science is to be recruited. The interim holder is the CSO, who also chairs the Protocol Review Board (R&R pp.4, 8). The same person would therefore review an exploration at Touchpoint 5 and chair its review at Touchpoint 6.

## Sources

- `context/analysis.md`, "The readiness reviewer has no owner"
- `context/Kind_Roles-and-Responsibilities_2026-09-28_v5.pdf`, pp.4, 8

## Open gaps

**The interim conflict isn't resolved.** R&R p.8 proposes an elected temporary member for the Head of Science seat on the Protocol Review Board while the CSO is interim. Whether that temporary member also makes the Kind-readiness determination hasn't been decided.
